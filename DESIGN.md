# Science Instrument Cabin

## Design sentence

An open research console used by a medically trained researcher at a quiet workstation after hours: dark instrument surfaces, cyan measurement traces, oxidized green status lights, and one signal-orange emphasis.

## Register

Bold, experimental, narrative, and precise. The visual reference is a scientific instrument panel and field notebook, not a cyberpunk HUD, SaaS dashboard, or editorial magazine.

## System

- Structural background: deep navy and ink black.
- Primary interaction: electric cyan.
- Secondary status: oxidized green.
- Attention state: signal orange.
- Type: a calm system sans for reading with a restrained mono face only for short technical labels.
- Shape: sharp 4-12px corners, thin rules, asymmetric bands, and content-led layouts.
- Motion: one hero signal trace plus purposeful section transitions. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Rules

- One dominant idea per viewport.
- No gradient text, decorative two-axis grids, purple-led gradients, or default glass cards.
- Numbers appear only where they communicate actual counts or an ordered route.
- Cards are used for repeated content or a dialog, never as nested page sections.
- Every external link keeps `noopener noreferrer`.
- Public content remains static and privacy-first.

# Product Direction

## Position

This is a public personal portfolio for a medically trained researcher who connects clinical questions, bioinformatics code, and AI tools. It is a public workbench, not a hospital service, clinical decision system, or private contact directory.

## Audience

- Researchers looking for practical research tools and reproducible workflows.
- Readers of the public WeChat writing collection.
- Developers and agents evaluating the author's open skills and projects.

## Primary jobs

1. Understand the person and the public boundary in one viewport.
2. Choose a useful lane: medical research, knowledge writing, or R graphics.
3. Browse selected publications and public WeChat articles without implying private or unverifiable metrics.
4. Open the relevant public repository or article search result.

## Content boundary

The public bio is limited to: "医学博士，985 临床医学本科，曾任肿瘤内科和介入科医生，爱好代码和 AI。" The site must not expose phone numbers, addresses, private email, credentials, backend links, local paths, patient data, or unpublished material.

## Non-goals

- No backend, tracking expansion, user accounts, or automatic audio playback.
- No claims of clinical advice, verified popularity, or private WeChat analytics.
- No visual competition between a mascot, a music player, a manifesto illustration, and the research content.
